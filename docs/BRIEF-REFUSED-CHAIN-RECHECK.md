# BRIEF: a first-attempt refusal invites a fresh signature on the facilitator's word (DEFERRED-REFUSED-CHAIN-RECHECK)

Status: PROPOSED, 5 Oct 2026, for Ben's ruling. Exchange: `exchange/REVIEW_refused-chain-recheck-brief-2026-10-05.md` (r1 amendments applied). Money path: any build goes through the D-018 Opus gate; the deploy is Ben's. Named in public by envoy 80 (1f3d9 note 28807, answering parallax 28208/28266) as "on our local deferred list ... not built, no date promised".

## The defect, re-derived from the code at `44f95d1a`

1. `refused` is reached from ONE place: payAndSettle's first `/settle`, on a rule-7 recorded refusal (`src/x402.ts:717-756`; `markRefused` has no other caller). Its answer is a 402 carrying `accepts` and the facilitator's words, "By its account no money moved." (`src/x402.ts:419`, `:749-755`). For `listing_pay` the route then releases the listing's reservation (the `ok: false` return carries no `keepReservation`).
2. A refusal on any LATER attempt while the chain reads the authorisation unused is not acted on: the claim stays pending (H2, `src/x402.ts:1087-1098`). The re-POST path already refuses to trust the facilitator alone; the first attempt does not.
3. A refused row is never re-read: the reconciler SELECT takes only `pending` and `settled_unbooked` (`src/settlement-reconcile.ts`, under the DEFERRED-REFUSED-CHAIN-RECHECK comment); an identical re-send is answered from the row (`src/x402.ts:867` -> `claimAnswer`, `src/settlement-claims.ts:633-639`), a 402 with `accepts` unless a concurrent facilitator success already stamped a contradiction.
4. The signed authorisation stays valid until its `validBefore`, which this server bounds to at most `now + PAYMENT_MAX_TIMEOUT_SECONDS (300) + PAYMENT_VALID_BEFORE_SKEW_SECONDS` at signing (`src/x402.ts:545-552`). The society cleared its copy (`rpc_body = NULL`), but the facilitator was sent one (`src/x402.ts:704-706`). If that authorisation is settled after the refusal (a wrong refusal, a facilitator retry, or an `insufficient_funds` refusal followed by the payer's balance arriving and someone submitting it), and the payer has meanwhile signed the fresh one we invited, both can move money. Nothing in the society would see the first; the refused answer carries no reporting pointer.

How likely: low. A 200 with `success: false` and a reason is what PayAI calls a recorded failure (`src/x402.ts:700-703`); whether it can ever settle that authorisation afterwards is not established either way. But the window is real, it is the only place left where our code invites a second signature without either the chain's proof or a settlement report, and the invariant parallax proposed (no re-authorisation once settlement evidence may exist) fails exactly here.

## Options

**A. Detect after the fact.** Keep `refused`. Add a one-time chain read for each refused row once `valid_before + RECONCILE_EXPIRY_MARGIN_SECONDS` has passed (the existing C6 two-RPC proof at a block past that time). `authorizationState` true means the nonce is USED, which is spent OR cancelled by its signer (`src/settlement-claims.ts:389-394`, `src/settlement-chain.ts`); it proves neither a transfer, nor its recipient, nor when it happened. So a used read stamps a marker in the stopped-message form (`src/settlement-claims.ts:576-585`): "the authorisation reads used; money may have moved; a person checks the chain by hand", served on the attention list and on every re-send; never a settlement contradiction and never a claim that a transfer happened. Unused at the proof block -> mark it checked so it is never read again. Runs in the 06:00 reconciler and on an identical re-send. Cost: one expiry-proof operation per refused row, once (up to eight RPC fetches, `src/settlement-chain.ts:94-96`); a marker; attention-list and served-text changes. Does NOT prevent the double payment, and for `listing_pay` the reservation was already released, so a second payer may have been booked against the listing; a person refunds by hand.

**B. Prevent (recommended).** Treat a first-attempt rule-7 refusal the way the re-POST path already treats every refusal: do not write `refused`; keep the claim `pending` with the facilitator's words through a write bound to the claim's take time exactly as R2-1 binds `markRefused` (`updated_at = takenAt`; today's `noteUnknown` has no such predicate and returns nothing, so B needs a bound variant that reports whether it wrote); on a write that changed nothing, re-read the claim and answer from its current state (`answerFromMovedClaim`), never a direct answer over a claim another holder may have settled; keep a `listing_pay` reservation (`keepReservation: true`), and answer WITHOUT `accepts`: the facilitator refused it and by its account no money moved; do not sign again yet; re-send this identical request after `<valid_before + margin, as a UTC time>` and you will be told whether it expired unused (sign fresh), settled, or remains unresolved or stopped for manual chain review if the expiry proof or the facilitator answer does not produce a safe verdict. The existing expiry proof (C6) then turns it `expired` on that re-send, or on a later 06:00 run when the row is selected and budget permits, and `expired` already invites the fresh signature with the chain's proof behind it. Cost: a genuinely refused payer cannot be told to sign again before the earliest eligible re-send time, `valid_before + RECONCILE_EXPIRY_MARGIN_SECONDS` (about eleven minutes after signing at most under the current constants, and often less: validBefore is bounded at `now + 300 + 60` seconds, plus the 300-second margin), and must re-send; resolution has no promised deadline (the expiry proof needs the chain's own block time past that point at two RPCs, and the 06:00 reconciler is budgeted and can shed rows, `src/settlement-reconcile.ts:143-150`); a refused listing payment holds its listing for that window (the gate's LOW-3 already accepts this on the re-POST path). Production `refused` becomes unreachable from new claims; existing refused rows (believed none in prod: no paid request has run since M2, Add 84 s11) keep today's answer. Parallax's invariant then holds by construction: nothing invites a fresh signature until the chain proves the first can no longer move money.

**Not recommended:** a per-reason split (B for `insufficient_funds`, A for the rest). Reason strings are the facilitator's and unversioned; a misclassified reason re-opens the hole.
