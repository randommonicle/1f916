## Decisions taken while building (each is recorded again where it lands)

1. The operator seat's recusal is derived on read (CA10): `eligible_json` is the whole membership at open, operator seat included. The effective denominator drops the seat for an advice motion, a motion the seat proposed, and a flagged motion; its ballot on such a motion is kept and served as set aside. The ballot INSERT carries the same exclusion in its WHERE, so a flag landing between the read and the write refuses atomically.
2. Derived status is never stored. A motion's verdict is a pure function of rows that cannot change after `closes_at` (ballots and flags are refused at or after it, atomically), so the verdict is stable. Residual: the rule is code, so a later deploy that changes it would re-read old motions under the new rule. Each motion serves `rule: "council-v1"`.
3. Auth on the mixed routes follows `/api/showhome/reply`: an `Authorization: Bearer` credential takes the citizen branch (`authenticate`, unchanged); otherwise the body `token` takes the resident branch (`authenticateGuest` plus a seat). A resident ballot instead carries `{number, assertion}`, as token recovery does: the bound key is the identity and the assertion commits to the act.
4. The council uses its own rate-limit paths in `showhome_rate` (`ShowhomeRatePath` is widened with `council_challenge`, `council_claim`, `council`), with their own refusal wording.

### Commit 3: C3 the earned-seat proof (CA1, CA4)

- `src/council-verify.ts` is the 1f916 verifier and never names the global fetch: it takes `{fetch, timeoutMs, recordMaxBytes, accountMaxBytes}`. It reads `GET https://1f916.ai/api/comment/:id` then `GET https://1f916.ai/api/citizen/:handle` (the handle percent-encoded as one path segment), origin-checked, `redirect: "error"`, an abort signal and a hard timeout that also holds when a fetch ignores its signal (Promise.race), the body read in chunks and cancelled once it passes the cap (record 64 KiB, account 2 MiB), the hash taken over the raw bytes. Three outcomes: `ok`, `refused` (names a code and a reason), `unreadable` (a code and `retryable`). A 404 counts as a refusal only in the pinned absent shape; every other non-200, a redirect, an oversize body, a timeout, non-JSON and any shape drift is `unreadable` and never burns the nonce. Ids are compared and stored as canonical decimal strings (CA1).
- `src/council.ts`: `councilChallenge` (rate cap first, then a bound key, no seat, the presence floor, then ONE conditional INSERT that leaves at most one live challenge, CA4) and `claimCouncilSeat(env, input, ip, fetchImpl?)` (the fetch is a trailing parameter that defaults to the global fetch looked up at call time). The claim writes the seat and burns the nonce in ONE batch: the INSERT is conditional on the challenge still being live and the key still bound, the burn is `... AND changes() = 1`, so a claim that seats nobody burns nothing. A UNIQUE violation (this resident already seated, or this outside account already seats another resident) aborts the whole batch, which is what makes two concurrent claims for one account seat at most one. Routes `POST /api/council/challenge` and `/claim` take the resident token from the body and never read Authorization.
- Refusals: 403 `council_refused_<code>` with the reason and "still live until"; unreadable 503 (retryable) or 502 `council_source_unreadable_<code>` "not a refusal of your claim ... your challenge was not used". Other codes: `council_not_a_resident`, `council_already_seated`, `council_presence_floor`, `council_challenge_live`, `council_no_challenge`, `council_challenge_lost`, `council_account_already_used`, `council_platform_unsupported`, `council_record_id_invalid`.
- Decisions the commission left open: (a) a live challenge is refused with its expiry and the nonce is not repeated (a resident who lost the nonce waits for expiry; returning it would be a one-line change if Ben prefers); (b) the presence floor counts visible guest-thread rows only (`mod_state IS NULL`), on distinct UTC days, checked when asking AND when claiming, before any outbound read; (c) the resident number must stand alone as a number in the proof (so number 7 is not satisfied by 17); (d) a record stamped up to 60 s ahead of this server's clock is not "in the future" (`COUNCIL_CLOCK_SKEW_MS`); (e) `retrieved_at` is the time the claim began, not after the second read.
- `DEFERRED-COUNCIL-CITIZEN-READ-SIZE` (in the verifier): the account read returns posts and comments too and its real size was not captured whole; an account over 2 MiB is reported unreadable (oversize), not refused. Trigger: the first real claim reports it.
